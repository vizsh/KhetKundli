# The season file (pitch)

Scope: `/`, the page judges and partners open first. Mode: Persuade. Inherits the Living Record world (DESIGN.md): manila board, ruled paper, typed / ballpoint / violet-stamp provenance, Anek + Courier Prime + Kalam + Tiro Devanagari Marathi.
Audience and action: Phaltan Hackathon judges (agriculture track) deciding in minutes whether KhetKundli is one integrated, real product. They should understand the offer from the first viewport, believe it through live proof, and open the desk and the phone.
Proof on hand: the live season engine, real OSM geography, live Open-Meteo weather, the on-device model, the synced phone-to-desk ripple. No testimonials, users, pilots or partnerships exist; none are claimed.
Constraints: every number comes from the running engine and is stamped LIVE or SIMULATED; English-first with Marathi alongside headings; works at 390 px.
Memorable moment: scrolling rolls the big date stamp through the season and the whole page re-inks to that day.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The pitch is the season itself: scrolling turns the date stamp from 1 June to 31 January and each chapter shows what the records knew that day. It refuses the hackathon default of a hero banner, three feature cards and a team slide.

OWN-WORLD: Inherited Living Record world: the manila file board as ground, sheets of ruled register paper for each chapter, the rolling-band date stamp at poster scale as the page's single moving instrument, survey-map linework, red-ink hatching, violet stamps, the yellow perforated slip for the ripple.

STORY: The judge reads one line on what KhetKundli is, scrolls through one season of one taluka (bahar, blight front, a washed-off spray, an offline scan that ripples, the harvest wave and the Pune glut, pooled runs and an honest sale slip, what the village kept), sees which six problem statements each part answers and what is live versus simulated, then opens the desk and Sunita's phone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 1440x900 on the manila board: wordmark top-left; top-right two stamp buttons, Open the Taluka Desk and Open Sunita's phone. Left half: headline "Every plot remembers. Every village sees together." with its Marathi line and one sentence on the mechanism. Right half: the date stamp at poster scale reading the season's first day, with a typed line "scroll to play the season". At 390 px the stamp sits under the headline and the buttons stack.

FORM: Day-by-day: the date stamp drives the scroll; position 6 on the ordered list of seven pitch structures; surface seed key cb0b49c1; lead of the dealt hand, built by the user's instruction to improvise. Raise from candidate 7 (six problem-statement file tabs): a strip mapping each source problem statement to the module that answers it. Signature interaction: scroll scrubs the shared season clock; the sticky stage swaps the chapter's visual (wheel, survey map, ripple slip, wave chart, sale waterfall) as the stamp rolls. Motion grammar: odometer digits, 200 ms re-ink, sheets slide like paper; nothing moves without the clock moving.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
